      Aetheria renders a real-time simulation of up to four million particles moving through a four-dimensional
      quaternion Julia attractor and an analytic curl-noise field. The fourth dimension is projected onto the screen
      through a rotating hyperplane, so dragging rotates the cloud in the XW, YW and ZW planes. Making a sound or
      enabling the procedural drone makes the nebula breathe with the low frequencies, shift its palette with the mid
      frequencies, and sparkle with the high frequencies. All controls in the panel are keyboard operable.
